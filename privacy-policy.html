Mobile Application Privacy & Data Protection Policy
Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Purpose

Shringar Jewellers ("we", "our", or "us") operates this mobile e-commerce application to provide users with certified gold, diamond, and silver jewellery browsing, real-time live bullion pricing, and direct doorstep order fulfillment.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected by our app, why it is collected, and how we safeguard user data in strict compliance with Google Play Store User Data & Privacy policies.

2. Information Collected & Features

Our app only collects data necessary to operate its core jewellery e-commerce features:

  • Account Registration Data: User's Full Name, 10-digit Mobile Number, Username, and Encrypted Password for secure account creation and login.
  • Delivery Address Information: Recipient Name, Mobile Number, Street Address, City, State, and PIN code used solely to dispatch and deliver jewellery orders.
  • Cart & Wishlist: Saved jewellery selections and ring/bangle customization sizes.
  • Order Records & Invoices: Items ordered, weight, metal purity (24K/22K/18K/Silver), total price, payment selection, and tracking numbers.
  • No Sensitive Financial Storage: We do NOT collect or store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs. All payments are processed through secure, bank-grade payment channels.
  • Zero Unnecessary Permissions: The app does not access your contacts, camera roll, microphone, or background GPS location.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Order Fulfillment: To process, hallmark, pack, and ship your jewellery pieces to your saved shipping address.
  • Order Tracking: To provide live order status updates (Pending, Confirmed, Hallmarking, Dispatched, Delivered) along with courier tracking numbers.
  • Account Security: To verify login sessions, maintain your cart/wishlist sync, and protect against fraudulent activity.
  • Customer Care: To respond to customer queries when initiated by the user via WhatsApp support.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Zero Advertising

  • No Data Sale: We strictly DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Logistics Couriers: Delivery address and recipient phone numbers are shared solely with authorized courier services (e.g. BlueDart, Sequel Logistics, SpeedPost) to complete parcel delivery.
  • Legal Compliance: Transaction invoices and GST tax records are maintained in accordance with applicable statutory Indian laws.

5. Security & Permanent Account Deletion

  • Password Cryptography: All user passwords are encrypted using salted cryptographic hashing (Bcrypt) prior to database storage.
  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All app-to-server data communications are protected with 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Account Deletion Rights: Store administrators can permanently delete customer accounts upon request from the Admin Panel. Upon deletion, all user credentials, personal data, and active sessions are permanently erased from MongoDB, and the user is immediately blocked from signing into the app.
  • Self-Request: Users can request immediate account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.
